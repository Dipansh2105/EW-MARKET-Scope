Project overview

A data-first view of electric mobility.

The project brings key EV market indicators together in one dashboard so users can explore how sales vary by geography, manufacturer and battery type.

🌍

Regional sales

Compare annual EV sales across major global market regions and identify relative market scale.

🔋

Battery technology

Explore the distribution of vehicle counts across LFP, NCA, NMC, Sodium-Ion and Solid-State batteries.

🏭

Manufacturer performance

Review annual sales units across EV manufacturers and compare their market presence.

⭐

Customer ratings

Inspect customer rating patterns by market region to add a customer perspective.

📊

Interactive exploration

Use dashboard filters and visual comparisons to explore the available dimensions.

💡

Market insights

Bring multiple indicators together to support discussion and data-driven interpretation.
